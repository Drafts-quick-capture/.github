# Drafts - Quick Capture Notepad for Turning Text into Actions

## About Drafts

Drafts is a focused capture workspace for people who think faster than they can organize. Drafts opens straight into a blank note, so a quick capture starts before distractions appear. From there, Drafts becomes a note taking home for short thoughts, meeting fragments, links, checklists, and longer writing that needs a calm place to grow.

Drafts treats text as the beginning of work, not the end. A captured line can stay a note, become a task, turn into a message, or feed a longer document. Drafts idea organizer habits form naturally because every entry stays editable, searchable, and ready to move. For users who rely on Drafts text actions, the path from a rough sentence to a finished result stays short and predictable.

Drafts also fits a writing workspace rhythm. Some sessions are pure capture, some are review, and some are shaping notes into something shareable. Drafts supports all three without forcing a rigid system. The Drafts writing workspace stays quiet, readable, and fast, which matters when inspiration arrives in a queue, a commute, or the middle of another task.
